Paneer Pakora


Paneer pakoda is a popular North Indian deep-fried snack made by coating spiced Indian cottage cheese in a savory gram flour batter. To prepare the base, cut 200 grams of fresh paneer into neat cubes, rectangles, or triangles, and optionally toss them with red chili powder, chaat masala, and garam masala or stuff a pocket inside each piece with tangy green coriander-mint chutney. In a separate mixing bowl, whisk together 1 cup of gram flour (besan), 2 tablespoons of rice flour or cornstarch for crispiness, carom seeds (ajwain), asafoetida (hing), turmeric, ginger-garlic paste, and salt. Gradually pour in water while stirring to create a smooth, thick, and lump-free batter that can easily coat the paneer without sliding off.

Heat neutral oil or mustard oil in a deep pan or kadai over medium flame. Dip each piece of paneer thoroughly into the prepared besan batter, ensuring a uniform coating. Gently drop the coated pieces into the hot oil in small batches to avoid lowering the oil temperature or overcrowding the pan. Deep-fry them for 3 to 4 minutes, turning occasionally, until the crust turns a crisp, golden-brown color. Remove the pakodas using a slotted spoon, drain the excess oil on a paper towel, sprinkle a pinch of chaat masala on top, and serve them piping hot with green mint chutney or tomato ketchup.

Ingredients

  • Gram flour (Besan)
  • Indian cottage cheese (Paneer)
  • Spices (Namak/Salt, Lal Mirch Powder/Red chili powder, Haldi/Turmeric)
  • Coriander powder (Sukka dhaniya)

Steps

  1. Prepare the Paneer: Cut the Indian cottage cheese (Paneer) into thick cubes or rectangles, and lightly sprinkle them with a pinch of salt and red chili powder.
  2. Make the Batter: In a mixing bowl, combine the gram flour (Besan), salt, red chili powder, turmeric (Haldi), and crushed coriander powder (Sukka dhaniya).
  3. Whisk with Water: Gradually add water to the flour mixture and whisk it thoroughly to create a smooth, thick batter that easily coats the back of a spoon.
  4. Heat the Oil: Pour cooking oil into a deep pan or kadhai and heat it over medium flame for deep frying.
  5. Coat the Paneer: Dip each seasoned paneer cube into the gram flour batter, ensuring it is completely and evenly coated on all sides.
  6. Fry Until Golden: Carefully drop the coated paneer into the hot oil and deep fry them, turning occasionally, until they turn crisp and golden-brown.
  7. Drain and Serve: Remove the pakodas using a slotted spoon, drain the excess oil on paper towels, and serve them hot with green chutney or tomato ketchup.
